# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance Lead defines the quality bar for the project and ensures that releases meet the agreed standards before production readiness. They work closely with engineering, product, and project leadership to drive quality gates and defect resolution.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality criteria, and release readiness standards
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Coordinate defect triage, prioritization, and validation with the delivery team
- Own quality gate reviews before major milestones or releases
- Monitor release quality trends and recommend corrective actions

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve test coverage and confidence in change quality
- Create predictable quality checks across the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Quality reviews and defect triage sessions
- Test plans and readiness assessments
- Release sign-off discussions with project and product leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to define test coverage, bug ownership, and validation steps.
- Collaborates with Product Managers to verify that acceptance criteria support customer outcomes and quality expectations.
- Reports quality risks and readiness status to Project Managers so delivery milestones and release plans reflect actual risk.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates the plan, execution, and communication of product releases. They ensure that deployment activities are sequenced correctly, dependencies are managed, and stakeholders understand timing and impact.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release calendars and deployment schedules
- Coordinate release readiness reviews with engineering, QA, and operations
- Communicate release status, change windows, and rollback plans
- Track deployment dependencies, risks, and post-release issues
- Support incident response and stabilization after release

### Goals
- Deliver releases with minimal disruption and high predictability
- Improve coordination across teams involved in deployment
- Reduce post-release defects and communication gaps

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment plans
- Executive status updates during launch windows
- Post-release reviews and incident summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers on build readiness and deployment sequencing.
- Works with the Quality Assurance Lead to confirm quality gates have been passed before launch.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of schedule dependencies and risk that could affect project milestones.
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer-facing release timing and communication readiness.

---

## Process Improvement Specialist

### Role Summary
The Process Improvement Specialist identifies friction in how teams work and drives practical improvements to project delivery practices. They turn lessons learned into clearer processes, better retrospectives, and more mature execution patterns.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze delivery patterns, bottlenecks, and recurring issues across teams
- Facilitate retrospectives and capture improvement opportunities
- Maintain and update team process documentation and checklists
- Help standardize templates, rituals, and handoff practices
- Track improvement actions and measure their effectiveness over time

### Goals
- Increase delivery efficiency and consistency
- Build a culture of continuous improvement
- Turn team lessons into repeatable, scalable processes

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective sessions and improvement workshops
- Process documentation updates and guideline reviews
- Metrics summaries and trend reporting to leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to improve planning, communication, and governance patterns.
- Partners with Developers and Product Managers to streamline handoffs and reduce rework.
- Uses Quality Assurance and Release data to identify systemic gaps in delivery quality and preparation.

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Manager ensures that the right people are informed, engaged, and aligned at the right time. They bridge business, delivery, and leadership groups to improve communication quality and reduce misunderstandings.

### Responsibilities
- Identify key stakeholders, priorities, and communication needs
- Maintain stakeholder maps and engagement rhythms
- Prepare and deliver updates for leadership, sponsors, and cross-functional partners
- Surface concerns, dependencies, and decisions that require escalation
- Support alignment across project goals, constraints, and decisions

### Goals
- Maintain trust and transparency with stakeholders
- Accelerate decision-making through clear communication
- Reduce confusion and conflicting expectations

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and steering committee updates
- Executive summaries and decision memos
- Cross-functional coordination across teams and leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to reflect customer and business priorities in stakeholder messaging.
- Works with Project Managers to ensure project status and risk communication is consistent and timely.
- Supports the Release Manager and Quality Assurance Lead by clarifying who needs release updates and when.

---

## Risk Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Risk Coordinator tracks project uncertainty, escalation paths, and mitigation progress. They help teams identify emerging risks early and ensure that risk information is visible to the right decision makers.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain risk registers and update risk status periodically
- Identify emerging dependencies, constraints, and issues with delivery impact
- Evaluate risk severity, probability, and mitigation options
- Escalate high-priority risks to project leadership and stakeholders
- Monitor mitigation actions and report progress over time

### Goals
- Reduce surprise events and missed milestones
- Strengthen proactive decision-making across the project
- Improve visibility into risk exposure and mitigation readiness

### Typical Communication
- Risk reviews and mitigation planning sessions
- Risk dashboards and summaries for leadership
- Escalation notes and dependency updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing schedule, dependency, and delivery risks.
- Works with Developers and QA to understand technical risk and validate mitigation plans.
- Coordinates with the Stakeholder Manager on escalation messaging and leadership awareness.
- Helps Product Managers understand trade-offs that may affect product outcomes or delivery commitments.
